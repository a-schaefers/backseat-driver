# Backseat Driver Commercial License

Backseat Driver is source-available. Anyone may read it, change it and share it under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 in `LICENSE`. That license covers personal use and use by noncommercial organizations. Commercial use needs this license instead.

## Do you need it?

You need a commercial license when a business uses Backseat Driver or has its people use it. That includes:

- employees or contractors using it for the business, on its time or its machines, whether on their work or to train: a team lead who has the team practice with it every Friday to keep their skills sharp needs a seat for each person on the team;
- a freelancer or consultant using it on paid work;
- using it as part of a paid product or service.

Learning counts. Training that a business asks for, pays for or makes time for is commercial use, even though it is education.

You do not need one for:

- your own learning, hobby projects and open source work done for no pay, on your own time and your own initiative, even if you have a day job;
- a school, university, charity, public research body or government institution, as `LICENSE` says.

When in doubt, it is honor system. Ask, or pick whichever feels fair.

## What it grants

A commercial license lets the licensee named on the license key use Backseat Driver for any commercial purpose, on as many seats as the key names, for as long as the key's term. A seat is one person using it. Everything else (changes, sharing, notices, no warranty, no liability) is as in `LICENSE`, with the commercial purpose added to the permitted purposes.

The license key is a receipt, not a lock. Backseat Driver keeps working when a key is missing, expired or cannot be checked, and only says so in its pane.

## Seats

A seat is one person, and one key covers all the seats bought with it. The license server counts the installs that check in with a key, each by a random number it picks for itself and nothing else. Installs are not people (one person may use several machines), so nobody using Backseat Driver is ever shown anything about seats. If, for weeks, far more installs use a license than it has seats, the person who bought it may get a short email suggesting more, at most every few months. Nothing ever stops working.
