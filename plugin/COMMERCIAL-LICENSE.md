## Do you need it?

You need a commercial license when Backseat Driver is used for a business: by its employees or contractors on its work, by a freelancer or consultant on paid work, or as part of a paid product or service.

You do not need one for:

- your own learning, hobby projects and open source work done for no pay, even if you have a day job;
- a school, university, charity, public research body or government institution, as `LICENSE` says.

When in doubt, it is honor system. Ask, or pick whichever feels fair.

## What it grants

A commercial license lets the licensee named on the license key use Backseat Driver for any commercial purpose, on as many seats as the key names, for as long as the key's term. A seat is one person using it. Everything else (changes, sharing, notices, no warranty, no liability) is as in `LICENSE`, with the commercial purpose added to the permitted purposes.

The license key is a receipt, not a lock. Backseat Driver keeps working when a key is missing, expired or cannot be checked, and only says so in its pane.
